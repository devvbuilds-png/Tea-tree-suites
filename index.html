Our Story

A Boutique Oasis
Above the Valley

Tea Tree Suites was conceived as Manipal's premier boutique retreat — a place where contemporary comfort and warm, attentive hospitality converge. Every room is designed to frame the natural beauty that surrounds us, from the dramatic valley vistas to the lush canopy of trees.

Whether you are here for medical care, academics, or simply to explore coastal Karnataka, we offer a calm, refined sanctuary to return to each evening.
